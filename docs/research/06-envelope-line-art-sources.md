# Botanical line-art picks

All files are single motifs with dark-grey (#2b2b2b) lines on transparency (PNG) or editable vector strokes/fills (SVG). PNGs were cropped tightly, white petal fills were removed, long side is 1000-1280 px. No file requires attribution.

Contact sheet: `sheet.jpg`.

Sources blocked in this environment: SVG Repo (HTTP 429) and PublicDomainVectors (HTTP 403), even via Chromium. Openclipart was reachable but its rose line drawings were heavier/traced than these.

# Bouquet line-art candidates (engagement envelope)

All files: transparent PNG, ink recoloured to dark grey #333333 (alpha = inverse luminance of the
white-background preview), 1280 px on the long side. Source previews are Pixabay's `_1280` renders;
no SVG originals are included because Pixabay only serves SVG/AI downloads to logged-in accounts.
`sheet.jpg` is the contact sheet (best pick first).

Licence for every file: **Pixabay Content License** (https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/) —
free for commercial use, no attribution required (credit is appreciated but optional). Three of the
alternates carry Pixabay's "AI generated" label; that does not change the licence, but is noted below.

## 1. BEST PICK — `bouquet-fineline-ranunculus_px6992182.png`
- Source: https://pixabay.com/vectors/bouquet-flowers-leaves-roses-6992182/
- Author: lyubmalee (Pixabay)
- Licence: Pixabay Content License — commercial use OK, attribution not required. Not AI-labelled.
- 1280 x 1271 px (about 1:1), fine single-weight continuous outline, no fills or hatching.
- Quality: the only true fine-line stationery drawing found — ranunculus/garden roses, lilies, buds,
  eucalyptus-style leaves, gathered at the base like a hand-tied bouquet. Caveats: hairline strokes
  (about 1.5 px at this size) print faint below ~300 dpi; one leaf is clipped flat at the left edge
  of the preview; no halo after conversion.
- `bouquet-fineline-ranunculus_px6992182_thickened.png` is the same drawing with a 1 px dilation
  (about 60 % blend) for printing at small envelope sizes. Use whichever reads better at the final size.

## 2. `bouquet-wildflower-posy_px9414333.png`
- Source: https://pixabay.com/vectors/flower-nature-bloom-flora-plant-9414333/
- Author: jkoets (Pixabay). Page shows Pixabay's "AI generated" label.
- Licence: Pixabay Content License — commercial use OK, attribution not required.
- 1119 x 1280 px (about 7:8). Daisies, cornflowers, grasses, gathered stems; upright posy.
- Quality: lovely silhouette and composition, but the stroke is uneven engraving-style with some
  hatching and small solid-black stem fills; reads heavier than the brief's single-weight ideal.

## 3. `bouquet-roses-outline_px8815292.png`
- Source: https://pixabay.com/vectors/rose-flower-plant-decorative-8815292/
- Author: GDJ (Gordon Johnson, Pixabay). Page shows Pixabay's "AI generated" label.
- Licence: Pixabay Content License — commercial use OK, attribution not required.
- 841 x 1280 px (about 2:3). Five roses plus a bud and leaves, stems tied at the base.
- Quality: very clean, perfectly uniform medium-weight outline with no fills; slightly clip-art /
  colouring-book in character, so less "premium" than #1 but technically flawless.

## 4. `bouquet-lily-cluster_px9414335.png`
- Source: https://pixabay.com/vectors/flower-nature-bloom-flora-plant-9414335/
- Author: jkoets (Pixabay). Page shows Pixabay's "AI generated" label.
- Licence: Pixabay Content License — commercial use OK, attribution not required.
- 1280 x 1267 px (about 1:1). Lilies/clematis with buds and curling leaves; loose spray rather than a tied bouquet.
- Quality: expressive brush-like outline with variable weight and some stipple detail; no vase.
  Good as a corner or flap motif; stems are not gathered.

## 5. `bouquet-simple-handtied_px10179177.png`
- Source: https://pixabay.com/vectors/bouquet-flowers-nature-bloom-jar-10179177/
- Author: Jozefm84 (Pixabay). Not AI-labelled.
- Licence: Pixabay Content License — commercial use OK, attribution not required.
- 871 x 1280 px (about 2:3). Daisies, clover heads and grasses, hand-tied.
- Quality: consistent single weight and a true bouquet shape, but naive/cute in style — closer to a
  children's-book illustration than fine stationery. Included only as a safe fallback.

## Rejected during review (for the record)
- Hundreds of Pixabay results were vases/urns, shaded engravings, wreaths/frames, colour clipart or
  single flowers. openclipart.org "bouquet" searches returned only colour clipart, vases and wreaths.
- freesvg.org search URLs now 404 (site changed), publicdomainvectors.org and svgrepo.com blocked
  curl (Cloudflare / HTTP 429), so no CC0 alternatives were obtained.
