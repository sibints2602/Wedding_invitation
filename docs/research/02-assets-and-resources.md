## 1. Typography (Google Fonts & Premium Free Sources)

### Recommended Font Pairings for Elegant Christian Weddings

#### Pairing 1: **Playfair Display + Lato** (Classic Elegance)
- **Display Font:** Playfair Display (Google Fonts)  
  - URL: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Playfair+Display
  - License: Open Font License (OFL) - Free for commercial use
  - Best for: Couple names, "Save the Date," section headers
  - Why it fits: High-contrast serif with subtle curves; reads as premium and traditional
  
- **Body Font:** Lato (Google Fonts)  
  - URL: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Lato
  - License: OFL - Free for commercial use
  - Best for: Invitation details, venue, date/time information
  - Why it fits: Clean, modern sans-serif; excellent readability at small sizes on mobile

#### Pairing 2: **Cormorant Garamond + Montserrat** (Sophisticated Modern)
- **Display Font:** Cormorant Garamond (Google Fonts)  
  - URL: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Cormorant+Garamond
  - License: OFL - Free for commercial use
  - Best for: Couple names, invitation title
  - Why it fits: High-contrast classic serif with elegant proportions; very wedding-appropriate
  
- **Body Font:** Montserrat (Google Fonts)  
  - URL: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Montserrat
  - License: OFL - Free for commercial use
  - Best for: Body text, details, RSVP information
  - Why it fits: Geometric sans-serif; modern yet elegant

#### Pairing 3: **EB Garamond + Jost** (Timeless Elegance)
- **Display Font:** EB Garamond (Google Fonts)  
  - URL: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/EB+Garamond
  - License: OFL - Free for commercial use
  - Best for: Main text, ceremony details
  - Why it fits: Old-style serif based on historical typeface; premium and literary feel
  
- **Body Font:** Jost (Google Fonts)  
  - URL: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Jost
  - License: OFL - Free for commercial use
  - Best for: Supporting text, RSVP, website navigation
  - Why it fits: Geometric sans-serif with personality; excellent web performance

#### Script/Accent Fonts (Use Sparingly)

- **Great Vibes** (Google Fonts)  
  - URL: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Great+Vibes
  - License: OFL - Free for commercial use
  - Why it fits: Flowing connected calligraphy; elegant but readable at large display sizes. Perfect for "Mr. & Mrs." or decorative initial capitals.
  - Mobile rendering: Excellent at 32px+; avoid below 24px

- **Tangerine** (Google Fonts)  
  - URL: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Tangerine
  - License: OFL - Free for commercial use
  - Why it fits: Delicate, graceful swashes; romantic feel. Best for short phrases like couple names.
  - Mobile rendering: Use at 48px+ for full elegance; swashes compress at smaller sizes

- **Pinyon Script** (Google Fonts)  
  - URL: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Pinyon+Script
  - License: OFL - Free for commercial use
  - Why it fits: Formal copperplate calligraphy; traditional and ornate. Ideal for formal, black-tie events.
  - Mobile rendering: Readable down to 28px

- **Dancing Script** (Google Fonts)  
  - URL: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Dancing+Script
  - License: OFL - Free for commercial use
  - Why it fits: Lively, energetic script with good readability. Great for informal sections or accent text.
  - Mobile rendering: Very mobile-friendly; readable at 16px+

- Limitation: **Cannot be downloaded and used on your wedding website without the Premium/paid license**
- Use only if: Music is embedded from YouTube (live stream, video player)

### Public Domain Hymn Recordings

#### Canon in D (Pachelbel) - Public Domain Composition
- **Composition:** Public domain (17th century)
- **Challenge:** Need free recording; many recordings are copyrighted
- **Solutions:**
  1. IMSLP (International Music Score Library Project): https://imslp.org
     - Search "Pachelbel Canon in D"
     - Download sheet music; hire musician or use free synthesizer
  2. Free video sites (YouTube): Search "Canon in D royalty free" or "Canon in D free download"
     - Verify license in description before download
  3. Incompetech: Search for "Pachelbel" or "classical canon" arrangements

#### Other Hymn Options
- Search: "royalty-free Christian hymn instrumental" on Pixabay or Incompetech
- Examples: "Amazing Grace," "Jesus Christ is Risen," "The Lord's Prayer"
- Verify: Check that the **recording** (not just composition) is free to use

### Recommended Setup

| Purpose | Source | Track Style | Duration |

| Ceremony processional | Incompetech or free Canon recording | Classical, elegant, 2-4 minutes | Single play |

## 9. Tools & Utilities

### Image & SVG Optimization

#### SVGOMG
- URL: https://svgomg.net
- Purpose: SVG optimization and minification
- Features:
  - Removes unnecessary metadata
  - Optimizes paths and curves
  - Applies compression techniques
  - Recolor SVGs in-browser
- Best for: Reducing SVG file size (10-30% typical savings)
- Cost: Free

#### Google Squoosh
- URL: https://squoosh.app
- Purpose: Image compression and format conversion
- Supports: PNG, JPG, WebP, AVIF
- Features:
  - Real-time before/after comparison slider
  - Batch processing
  - Quality/file-size tradeoff control
  - Runs locally in browser (no upload to server)
- Best for: Optimizing texture backgrounds, couple photos
- Cost: Free

### Icon & Favicon Generation

#### Favicon Generator (RealFaviconGenerator)
- URL: https://realfavicongenerator.net
- Purpose: Create favicon from image or vector
- Output: Generates favicon.ico, Apple touch icons, manifest.json for PWA
- Best for: Professional favicon set in one upload

#### Figma (Free tier)
- URL: https://figma.com
- Tools: Batch export, SVG optimization
- Can also: Design custom icons, test on devices
- Cost: Free tier sufficient for small wedding site

### OG Image Generation

- Purpose: Preview and generate OG meta tags for social sharing

## 12. Project Implementation Recommendations

### Phase 1: Design Setup
1. Choose font pairing (suggest: Playfair Display + Lato)
2. Download free textures (gold foil + cream linen)
3. Test color palette against reference site
4. Download 5-10 floral SVGs from Pixabay/SVGRepo for testing

### Phase 2: Asset Assembly
1. Source couple placeholder photos (Unsplash)
2. Download Christian symbol set (Christicons)
3. Select 2-3 Lottie animations (envelope, confetti, rings)
4. Choose background music (Pixabay or Incompetech)
5. Optimize all SVG assets with SVGOMG

### Phase 3: Technical Integration
1. Load Google Fonts via `<link>` or `@import`
2. Optimize all images with Squoosh (target: <50KB per image)
3. Inline small SVGs; lazy-load larger assets
4. Use CSS custom properties (--gold, --ivory) for maintainability
5. Test music playback and animation timing on mobile

### Phase 4: Final QA
1. Check favicon looks good across browsers
2. Test OG image preview with Metatags.io
3. Verify all assets load in 3G connection (Lighthouse audit)
4. Test animations on Safari/older iOS devices
5. Verify attribution links work (if applicable)
