## D. Illustrations & Custom Watercolor Production

### Important Note: Reference Site Illustrations
The reference site (myshaadhilink.in/invitation/christian-elegance) appears to use **AI-generated illustrations** for:
- The couple walking down the church aisle (watercolor style)
- Single watercolor rose petals
- Floral corner sprays

### 3 Practical Ways to Produce Custom Matching Illustrations

#### 1. **AI Image Generation + Background Removal** (Fastest, ~$0-50)
- **Generate watercolor illustration:**
  - Use Claude AI's image generation, Midjourney, DALL-E, or Gemini to create a custom watercolor couple illustration with prompt like: *"Watercolor painting of a couple walking down a church aisle, soft blush and cream colors, rose petals falling, dreamy aesthetic, loose brushstrokes"*
  - Cost: ~$0-20 (if using paid tier) or free with free trials

- **Remove background:**
  - Upload generated image to **remove.bg** (https://www.remove.bg/)
  - Or use free alternatives:
    - **Erase.bg** – https://www.erase.bg/ (free, no signup)
    - **Fotor Background Remover** – https://www.fotor.com/features/background-remover/
    - **Pixlr Remove Background** – https://pixlr.com/remove-background/
    - **Adobe Express** – https://www.adobe.com/express/feature/image/remove-background/
    - **Pixelcut** – https://www.pixelcut.ai/background-remover/ (free, no signup)
    - **BgEraser** – https://bgeraser.com/ (free, up to 20 images)
  
- **Result:** Transparent PNG ready for your website
- **Total Time:** 15-30 minutes
